The problem

The spreadsheet got you this far

In most churches the information is scattered: the member list in a spreadsheet, the cash book on paper, the tithes in a notepad and the receipts in somebody's phone. When the accountability meeting comes, someone spends a weekend putting it all together again.

Portal da Igreja brings those controls into one system, built for people who are not from a technical background: in the cloud, on a computer or a phone, with nothing to install.

Built in Brazil

Why a Brazilian system, abroad

Portal da Igreja was made for Brazilian churches, and that is exactly why it fits a Brazilian congregation overseas: the system speaks Portuguese, giving works through Pix into the church's own Brazilian account, and support answers in Portuguese over WhatsApp.

If your congregation collects locally — in dollars, euros or yen — you can still record those entries by hand in the finance module; what the system automates is Pix. Talk to us before subscribing so we can tell you honestly whether it fits.

Frequently asked

Questions from churches considering us

Is the system available in English?

Not yet. The software interface is in Portuguese, and so is support. The English pages exist so Brazilian churches abroad can find us; the product itself speaks Portuguese.

Can I pay in dollars or euros?

No. Subscriptions are charged in Brazilian reais (BRL) by card or boleto, through Stripe. An international card usually works, with the currency conversion your bank applies.

Can we bring the list of members we already have?

Yes. The import assistant reads Excel (.xlsx, .xls) and CSV files, suggests which field each column maps to and shows everything for you to review before saving. Duplicates are blocked by document number, e-mail or name.

How many people from our team can use it?

As many as the church needs, on any plan. Pastor, treasurer and secretary get separate logins with their own permissions. The plan limit is on registered members, not on users.
