Where it lives

The address of your church site

Once published, the site is available at a Portal da Igreja address, in the form app.portaldaigreja.com.br/yourchurch/portal. That is the link the church shares on Instagram, on WhatsApp and in print.

If your church already owns a domain, talk to us on WhatsApp: connecting a domain is arranged case by case with our team.

Being straight with you: these church sites are built for visitors who already have the link, not for search engines. They do not carry per-church meta tags or a sitemap, so do not expect the site alone to make your church appear in Google results.

Included in the plan

Nothing extra to buy

The website is included in every paid plan — Essencial, Crescimento and Avançado — at no additional cost and with no separate hosting. On the free plan the editor is locked; the rest of the system stays available.

Because the site and the management system are the same product, the calendar, the photos and the Pix key are entered only once.

Frequently asked

Questions from churches considering us

Do I need to know how to code, or hire someone?

No. You pick the blocks, write the text, upload the images and publish from the same panel you use to manage the church.

Is the website really included?

Yes, in every paid plan, at no extra cost. Only the free plan is without the site editor.

Can we use the domain our church already owns?

Connecting your own domain is arranged case by case with our team. Talk to us on WhatsApp before subscribing.

Will the site help our church show up on Google?

Not on its own. These sites are rendered for visitors rather than for search engines, and they carry no per-church meta tags or sitemap. Treat it as the page you share, not as a search strategy.
