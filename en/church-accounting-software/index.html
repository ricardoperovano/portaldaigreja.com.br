Treasury

The whole month in one place

Income and expenses

Every entry with its date, amount, category and description, split between money in and money out, and totalled automatically for the period you choose.

Your church's categories

A list of categories per kind, which you rename and organise. Renaming updates the older entries too; merging two categories merges their history.

Recurring fixed bills

Rent, salaries, power and internet become a weekly, monthly or yearly template. The system posts them on the right date every time, and you can pause or end them whenever you want.

Monthly closing as a PDF

Money in, money out, the comparison with the previous month, income by contribution type, expenses by category with each one's share, the largest outflows and what is already expected for the next 30 days.

Report by category

Pick the categories and campaigns you need to account for and get a report with every entry and its subtotals, as a PDF with space for signatures or as an Excel file.

Export for the accountant

Entries, contributions, campaigns and members export to CSV, which opens in Excel and in any accounting system.

Being clear about limits

What does not exist yet

So there is no surprise after you subscribe: the system does not do automatic bank reconciliation, does not store receipt attachments, has no accounts payable with due-date clearing and no budget by cost centre. What it does is entries, categories, recurring bills and reports.

It also does not replace your accountant and issues no tax documents. Amounts are recorded in Brazilian reais.

Frequently asked

Questions from churches considering us

Does the system post fixed bills by itself?

Yes. Mark a bill as recurring, with its frequency and day, and the entry is created automatically on the right date. If you add one whose start date has passed, the overdue entries are posted straight away.

Can I get a report for the board meeting?

Yes. The monthly closing comes out as a PDF with letterhead, numbered pages and the comparison with the previous month. The report by category also has signature lines.

Who on the team can see the finances?

Only people with administration or management permission. Operators and members have no access to the treasury.

Which currency does it use?

Brazilian reais. Entries in another currency can be recorded, but the system does not convert currencies.
