How it works

From the envelope to the report

💝

Four kinds of entry

Tithe (tied to a member), offering, anonymous plate offering — the one with no name attached — and donation to a campaign. Each lands in the right totals.

📱

Pix with a QR code

You register the church's Pix key and the system produces the QR code and the copy-and-paste code. The member picks an amount in the portal and pays from their banking app; the money goes straight to the church account, with no middleman.

✅

Confirmed by the treasurer

A Pix sent by a member stays pending until someone from the treasury checks the bank statement and confirms it. Nothing reaches the reports before that check, and every request carries the identifier that shows up on the statement.

🧾

Annual giving statement

In the portal, members see what they gave during the year and download the statement as a PDF, which spares the treasurer most of the questions.

🎯

Campaigns with a goal

A building, missions or outreach campaign with a goal and live progress, plus a report for that campaign alone.

📊

Who gave, and who stopped

A report for the leadership shows who gave in the period, who gave for the first time and who used to give and stopped — a pastoral care list, for internal use.

Being honest about Pix

What this is, and what it is not

Portal da Igreja is not a bank and does not process payments: the Pix code is generated from the church's own key and the money never passes through us. That also means there is no automatic reconciliation — someone from the treasury checks the statement and confirms, which keeps control with the church.

Pix is a Brazilian payment method and requires a Brazilian bank account. If your congregation collects in dollars, euros or yen, those entries can be recorded by hand in the finance module, but the system will not generate codes for them.

Frequently asked

Questions from churches considering us

Do we need a card reader or a payment company?

No. Pix uses the church's own key and the money lands in the church account. There is no middleman between the member and the church.

Does Pix work outside Brazil?

Pix is a Brazilian system and needs a Brazilian bank account for the church. Members abroad can pay it from a Brazilian banking app. Giving in local currency can be recorded manually.

What about offerings with no name on them?

There is an "anonymous offering" type for exactly that. It counts towards the church's offering totals without inventing a member.

Who can see how much each person gave?

Only people with administration or management permission. The report shared with the congregation shows totals only, with no names.
